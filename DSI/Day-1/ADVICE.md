# TIPS FOR SUCCESS

- `Tip 1`: Do the exercises, don't just watch along.

- `Tip 2`: Keep notes or a notebook. I ended up with a 282-page Microsoft Word file with screenshots, code snapshots, console results, and personal notes that I plan to refer to in the future.

- `Tip 3`: Complement what you are learning with an LLM. I regularly used Copilot chats to fill in the blanks where needed or get a "second opinion". That wasn't available years ago, and it's a huge advantage while taking a course like this today. You will find Andrew is telling you the truth!

If you're here, know that you made a great choice.

There are many ways to get educated on everything in this course. I have done multiple online coding courses, read two AI books, and audited university postgraduate programs taught from a theoretical perspective - DSI is a comprehensive, practical launching pad to whatever you want to do next!

In Data Science, you are forever learning, which, interestingly, is exactly what Andrew means by "infinity" in the course title.

If you read this note, good luck, and I hope you have fun along the way.
