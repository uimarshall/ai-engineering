## Filters in Tableau

Filters are used to limit the data shown in a worksheet, dashboard, or view. They help you focus on only the records that matter, such as a specific date range, region, product, or category.

Common filter types include:

- `Dimension filters` for text or categorical fields
- `Measure filters` for numeric values
- `Date filters` for specific time periods
- `Context filters` for improving performance and controlling filter order

## Pages in Tableau

Pages split a view into a series of separate pages based on a field. Instead of showing all marks at once, Tableau shows one subset at a time and lets you step through them like a slideshow.

Pages are useful when you want to:

- Compare data across categories one at a time
- Animate changes over time
- Reduce clutter in a crowded visualization

## Filters vs Pages

Filters remove data from the view. Pages keep the data split into multiple views so you can browse through it one page at a time.
